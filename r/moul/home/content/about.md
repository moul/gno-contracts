> [!NOTE]- About this page
> It is not a site. It is a [gno.land](https://gno.land) realm, and every
> paragraph above is a `Set` transaction against a key-value store living on
> chain.
>
> The layout is an entry in that same store, so the *shape* of the page, which
> sections exist and in what order, changes with a transaction rather than a
> redeploy. Nothing here is a template rendered somewhere else and uploaded: the
> front end is the contract's own `Render` function, and what you are reading is
> its return value.
>
> [Read the slots](/r/moul/home:slots) to see the page as the data it is, or the
> [source](https://github.com/moul/gno-contracts/tree/main/r/moul/home).
